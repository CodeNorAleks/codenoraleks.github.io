The Mine

Mining strength with iron

Free strength & meal planner. Drag your week together, log sets fast, get calories and protein from your own numbers, and a shopping list priced at Kiwi, Meny and Coop. Works offline. Nothing is uploaded.

Open The Mine
Put it on your home screen iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android: open in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app.
PC: Chrome/Edge → install icon in the address bar.
First time in, tap the pencil (Settings) and enter weight, height, age and goal — the app calculates the rest.
